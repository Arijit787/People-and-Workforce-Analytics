# People & Workforce Analytics Dashboard

**Tools:** Excel, MySQL, Power BI, Power Query, DAX

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze workforce composition, departmental distribution, salary, job satisfaction, business travel, and employee attrition. The solution transforms HR data into actionable insights to support workforce planning, retention analysis, and data-driven HR decisions.

## What This Dashboard Helps Analyze

- **Workforce Composition:** Analyze 1,470 employees by department, gender, and average age to understand workforce distribution.
- **Employee Attrition:** Examine attrition patterns by gender and explore workforce characteristics associated with employee turnover.
- **Job Satisfaction:** Compare satisfaction ratings across job roles to identify areas that may warrant further investigation.
- **Salary & Compensation:** Monitor total salary and salary hike distribution across workforce segments.
- **Business Travel:** Analyze employee travel frequency to understand workforce mobility patterns.
- **Departmental Analysis:** Compare employee distribution across Research & Development, Sales, and Human Resources.

## Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of workforce KPIs, helping HR teams:

- Monitor workforce composition and departmental staffing patterns.
- Identify attrition trends and potential employee retention concerns.
- Explore job satisfaction and compensation patterns across employee groups.
- Support workforce planning and evidence-based HR decisions.
- Reduce manual reporting effort through Power Query transformations and DAX-based metrics, with a reported 40% reduction in report-generation time.
